Active moves

Drag to reorder. The order is the order of the move buttons in battle.

    Known moves · 9

    Drag one onto a slot to use it

    Desktop: drag a slot by its handle to reorder, or drag a known move onto a slot to replace it (the replaced move goes back to the known moves). Keyboard and touch: the up and down arrows reorder, and Swap opens the picker. "STAB" marks damaging moves of the Pokémon's own type (x1.5 damage).

    Replace Flamethrower

    Pick a known move for slot 1. A move already in another slot swaps places.

    Teach a TM

    The TM is used up. The move joins Shiny Charmeleon's known moves.

    No TMs match your search.

    Teach Ice Beam to Shiny Charmeleon? All 4 slots are full, so it goes to the known moves; swap it in when you want it.

    TMs are named by code and move ("TM 395 Ice Beam") and sorted by code. Each option shows the move's type badge. The search matches the number or move name; the type chips filter by move type (only types the player has TMs of); both combine. The list scrolls inside the dialog.

    Evolve Shiny Charmeleon

    Choose an evolution. Level, boost, shine and known moves are kept.

    Names always use the display name: a shiny Pokémon is "Shiny Charmeleon" and evolves into "Shiny Charizard"; a regular one is "Charmeleon" into "Charizard". Species with several evolutions (Eevee, Gloom) list one row each. Rows that can't be chosen yet are disabled with the reason: "Needs a Water Stone", "Needs level 100".

    Shiny Charmeleon → Shiny Charizard

    Evolve Shiny Charmeleon into Shiny Charizard?

    This can't be undone. Charizard's base moves join the known moves.

    Remove Shiny Charmeleon from your party?

    It goes to your Box. You can add it back any time.

    Your last party Pokémon can't be removed: the button is disabled with a tooltip ("Your party needs at least one Pokémon").

    Your party is full

    Choose a Pokémon to send to the Box. Shiny Charmeleon takes its place.

    The same dialog is used from the Box: a card's menu gets "Add to party", which opens it when the party is full.

    Added to party

    Shiny Charmeleon joined your party.

    Lock Shiny Charmeleon?

    A locked Pokémon can't be released or traded until you unlock it. You can still battle with it, train it and use items on it.

    Unlocking needs no confirmation: it removes a protection and can be undone at once.

    Release Shiny Charmeleon?

    It leaves for good and you get nothing back.

    This Pokémon is shiny and has +14 boost. The boost stones used on it are lost too.
