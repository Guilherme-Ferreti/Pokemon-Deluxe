Order: the lobby slots and the run's team list are numbered (1 leads) and can be reordered by dragging (desktop) or with the "Move earlier" / "Move later" arrows (everywhere). In the lobby the order is sent with "Start run"; during a run each change is saved right away and applies from the next floor (not during a Tower battle). A fainted Pokémon can sit anywhere: the first one with HP left goes out first. The team is a frozen copy taken when the run starts: levels, boosts and moves gained elsewhere count from the next run. Between floors you can explore, battle trainers or gyms; while the run is active its 6 Pokémon can't be traded, released or evolved. A run is saved on the server: leaving and coming back shows it again. The battle page's end screen for Tower battles has "Back to the Tower".
