Four battles, then the Champion, in order, with your party in party order. Your party is healed after each battle. Losing or running ends the run. Beating the Champion pays ₽ 30,000.

Start the Elite Four Continue: battle Agatha Challenge again You earned ₽ 30,000 for beating Champion Blue. Every win of a new run pays it again.

Badge order is free. Leaders bring 2 to 6 Pokémon (here Brock 2, Giovanni 6); the rule, the team picker and the Challenge button follow the leader's team size. When the leader brings 6, you still pick all six: the picking order is the sending-out order. You can bring fewer than the leader (Challenge then asks to confirm), never more: once you've picked as many as the leader brings, the other rows are disabled. A party smaller than the leader's team can still challenge. Gym leaders and badge names are placeholders for the content in task 6.2.
