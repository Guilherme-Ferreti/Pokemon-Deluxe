    The existing Party page keeps its card of six square slots; each slot now shows its place in the sending-out order (1 leads, marked "Leads"), the portrait, display name, level and boost, types, and two arrow buttons. Each change is saved right away with no saving or saved text; errors use a toast. The slot links to the Pokémon page; the arrows don't. Empty slots link to the Box.
